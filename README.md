## The short version

Money does not collect, send or sell your information. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and the app never connects to your bank.

## What the app stores, and where

- **Your accounts, transactions, payees, categories, schedules and settings** are stored on your iPhone, inside the app. You enter them yourself or import them from files you choose (for example a QIF statement or a CSV list from Files or iCloud Drive).
- **If you turn on sharing**, the app writes your changes to an iCloud Drive folder that you choose, so your other devices (a second phone, or the computer version) can read them. That folder is stored by Apple in your iCloud account under Apple's terms. We have no access to it.
- **Appearance and sharing choices** (light or dark, accent colour, which folder) are stored on the device.

## What we receive

Nothing. The developer has no server for this app and receives no data from it.

If you test through TestFlight, Apple provides the developer with TestFlight information such as your email address (if you were invited by email), the app version, and any feedback or screenshots you choose to send. That is governed by Apple's privacy policy.

## Sharing, selling and tracking

We do not share your information with anyone, do not sell it, and do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

## Keeping and deleting your data

Your data stays until you delete it. Deleting the app deletes the data stored inside it on that phone. Files the app has written to an iCloud Drive folder you chose stay there until you delete them in the Files app.

## Children

Money is a personal finance tool and is not directed at children.
